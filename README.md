# Mert Aydin

Control & Automation Engineer at Komatsu Mining, based in Bowral, NSW, Australia.

I test, commission, and troubleshoot control systems on underground mining machinery: PLC-based platforms, distributed I/O, and safety-related control functions, tied together over EtherNet/IP, TCP/IP, and CAN bus. I build the test tooling and validation workflows around them in C, C++, and Python.

Before that I built autonomous robots and soft robotic systems as a research assistant at the University of Wollongong, with publications in soft robotics and haptics. Starting a Master of Science in Computer Science at Georgia Tech (OMSCS) in Fall 2026.

## Projects

- [GIM6010-48 Motor Calibration Tool](https://github.com/mrtndy/Steadywin-GIM6010-48-Calibration-Tool): desktop tool for calibrating and tuning GIM6010-48 gear motors over RS485 and CAN bus, with guided PI tuning, step-response tests, and live telemetry plotting
